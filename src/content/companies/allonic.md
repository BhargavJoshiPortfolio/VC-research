## What it does
A manufacturing platform that builds robot bodies by creating a skeletal scaffold, weaving soft load-bearing fibres around it and integrating actuators, tendons, sensors and wiring during production, instead of assembling many separate parts. [C]

## Problem
AI-driven robot software has outpaced the slow, manual manufacturing of robot hardware, and that gap limits the industry, according to one of its investors.

## Team
Benedek Tasi, Dávid Pelyva and David Holló, founded 2025. Founder backgrounds were not available in the coverage found so far.

## Round
€6M ($7.2M) pre-seed on 10 February 2026, reported as Hungary's largest pre-seed round. Led by Visionaries Club with Day One Capital, Prototype, SDAC Ventures, TinyVC and angels including people from OpenAI and Hugging Face, plus ETH Zurich and Northwestern University.

## Why it is on the list
A manufacturing-process bet that could benefit any robot maker, backed by strong signals from the international AI and robotics community.

## Open questions for the deep dive
No customers, pilots or metrics disclosed. Founder track record unknown. Whether braided bodies match conventional parts on durability and precision, and whether robot makers outsource body manufacturing at all.
