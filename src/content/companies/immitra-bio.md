## What it does
A platform combining mutation-agnostic gene editing, a digital target-identification system and non-viral delivery, aiming to avoid ex-vivo cell manipulation, patient conditioning and stem cell transplants. The lead asset, IB-003, targets an inherited anaemia. [C]

## Problem
Current curative gene therapies for blood disorders are complex, costly and hard on patients. A single-dose in-vivo approach could reach far more people.

## Team
Jan Nelis (CEO and co-founder), Amir Taheri (CSO) and Prof. Jacob Corn (scientific advisor).

## Round
CHF 2.4M (about $3M) upsized pre-seed on 21 July 2026: CHF 2.25M from venture and private investors plus CHF 150K non-dilutive. Led by Backbone Ventures, co-led by OCCIDENT. Proceeds fund preclinical proof-of-concept for IB-003.

## Why it is on the list
A platform-plus-lead-asset story in one of the most contested areas of biotech, with a science-led team and a milestone-driven use of funds.

## Open questions for the deep dive
No preclinical data in the announcement. Competition from well-funded in-vivo editors. Delivery is the hard problem, so how the non-viral approach performs matters most. CHF 2.4M is thin runway, so a seed round is likely soon. HQ city differs between sources.
