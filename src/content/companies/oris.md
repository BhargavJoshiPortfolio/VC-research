## What it does
Laser-based wireless power transmission, aiming at an in-orbit energy distribution grid for satellites and dual-use applications. [C]

## Problem
Satellites need more power. Solar panels degrade over time and produce nothing during eclipse, limiting mission life and payload.

## Team
Four Politecnico di Torino engineers: Andrea Villa (CEO), Anna Mauro (CTO), Domenico Edoardo Sfasciamuro (CCO) and Francesco Lopez (COO).

## Round and traction
€4.5M pre-seed in July 2026 led by Earlybird, co-led by Pitchdrive, plus a €0.5M regional grant. Powered a hovering drone over 100+ metres on the ground, completed a lunar wireless-power feasibility study for Thales Alenia Space, and validated a drone-charging platform in NATO's DIANA accelerator. A flight model is due for a 2027 in-orbit demonstration with DCUBED. [R]

## Why it is on the list
A strong lead investor, NATO and Thales validation, a clear demonstration milestone and dual-use pull from defence.

## Open questions for the deep dive
Power-transfer efficiency and beam safety. The regulatory path for lasers in orbit. Who buys first: satellite operators, defence or space agencies. Binary risk on the 2027 demonstration.
