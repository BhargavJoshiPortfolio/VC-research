## What it does
Pulls supplier documents, lab results and regulatory requirements into one system, with AI agents for certificate tracking, specification checks and incident recording. [C]

## Problem
Food quality teams run on spreadsheets, documents and email. Data is scattered across departments, so compliance gaps are hard to spot.

## Team
Louis Opsomer, Siska Lannoo and Julien Steel. Founded 2025. Backgrounds not yet established.

## Round and traction
€4M pre-seed on 3 September 2026, led by Pitchdrive with PROfounders Capital, Passion Capital, 100IN and Belgian food-industry investors Agristo and Darta. Used across the food chain in Belgium, the UK and the Netherlands. Customer names and counts not disclosed. [R]

## Why it is on the list
Strategic food-industry investors on the cap table, and compliance software tends to have sticky recurring revenue. A true foodtech pre-seed in a year when most food money went to later stages.

## Open questions for the deep dive
Customer numbers and revenue. Competition from incumbents. How much of the value is AI versus workflow software. This is the software end of foodtech, not alternative protein or ingredients.
