## What it does
A bioprinting platform that prints fibre-reinforced "gel-textile" composites, which the company says are up to 20 times stronger than standard bioprints while staying compatible with living cells. The longer-term aim is printers that hospitals use to make implants on demand. The first product in development is a ligament implant. [C]

## Problem
Millions of patients a year need grafts for mechanically demanding tissues, and donor shortages and graft failure limit recovery.

## Team
Dylan Yeo (Oxford) and Thomas Groom (Imperial College London). Founded 2025. Part of the SOSV IndieBio programme.

## Why it is on the list
A genuine medtech and regenerative-medicine play with a US lead investor (Draper Associates). Hospital point-of-care manufacturing is a distinctive angle.

## Open questions for the deep dive
No preclinical or clinical data found yet. The regulatory route for an implant combined with cell-containing material is demanding. Mechanical claims are the company's own. The announcement date still needs confirming.
