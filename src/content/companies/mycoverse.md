## What it does
Biological crop protection using fungi in place of chemical pesticides. An AI-driven platform identifies high-performing fungal strains, and in-house fungal production technology turns them into products. [C]

## Problem
Potato late blight is among Europe's most destructive crop diseases. Regulation is reducing chemical pesticide use and resistance to existing products is growing. The company cites a roughly $1.9B global opportunity. [C]

## Team
Svend Petersen (CEO) and Niels Bjerg Jensen. A spin-out of the Technical University of Denmark (DTU).

## Round and traction
€2.4M pre-seed on 3 March 2026, co-led by Future Food Fund and High-Tech Gründerfonds, with PINC (Paulig's venture arm). About €4.3M raised in total including an earlier €1.9M from the BioInnovation Institute. Lead candidates performed strongly in greenhouse trials; two years of field trials are planned. [R]

## Why it is on the list
Food-supply sustainability with real science behind it, a strategic food-industry investor and a clear regulatory tailwind.

## Open questions for the deep dive
Greenhouse results may not carry to the field. Biologicals have a history of inconsistent efficacy and slow registration under EU rules. Route to market and distribution. Some sector overlap with agtech software such as Tellia.
