## What it does
A single API into 1,000+ European brands for the prepaid market: brand-funded cashback, digital employee benefits (Germany's tax-free monthly benefit) and instant digital gift cards. [C]

## Problem
Prepaid infrastructure is fragmented. Partners have to integrate several vendors across issuing, brand relationships, settlement and compliance.

## Team
Achim Bönsch, Sebastian Seifert and Andreas Veller. All three built Barzahlen/viafintech, which was sold to NYSE-listed Paysafe in a reported nine-figure deal in 2021. [R]

## Round and traction
€3.4M pre-seed in March 2026, led by Motive Partners and seed+speed Ventures. About six months after launch it is live with the payment app Flizpay (average 5% brand-funded cashback) and HR platforms Recardy and Paylo. The company cites a €1.2 trillion market. [C]

## Why it is on the list
Proven founders with a prior exit in the same payments neighbourhood, and a specialist fintech lead investor with US and UK reach.

## Open questions for the deep dive
Thin margin structure in prepaid and gift cards. Regulatory dependence on the German employee-benefit rule. Whether "Stripe for prepaid" scales beyond Germany. The €1.2 trillion figure needs a source.
