## What it does
A manufacturer-neutral network for shared depot charging in electric road freight. Logistics companies open unused charging points at their depots to other fleets. Depotcharge handles marketing, access control, invoicing and payments, and depot owners set price and availability. The operating company is Evolve Energy GmbH. [R]

## Problem
Reportedly more than 60% of depot charging capacity sits unused, while carriers depend on expensive public charging. [C]

## Team
Joscha Specks (CEO) and Julius Wilhelm (CPTO), both with 20+ years in e-mobility and earlier work on Digital Charging Solutions, a BMW, Mercedes-Benz and BP joint venture. Marc Clausing joins as CCO.

## Round and traction
€2.7M pre-seed on 15 September 2026, led by High-Tech Gründerfonds. Reports 200+ registered companies and about 600 electric trucks, active in Germany, Austria, Switzerland and the Benelux. Distribution partners include BGL (about 7,000 member companies), E.L.V.I.S. AG and ASTAG. [C]

## Why it is on the list
An asset-light marketplace with real network traction and industry-association distribution, addressing a commonly cited barrier to truck electrification.

## Open questions for the deep dive
Take rate and revenue. Whether utilisation is high enough to motivate depot owners. Competition from public networks and fleet-charging players. Which side of the marketplace is the constraint. Transferability beyond DACH.
