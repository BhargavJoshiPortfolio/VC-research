## What it does
Describes itself as Europe's first Humanoids-as-a-Service platform. A monthly subscription covers robot selection, task training, IT integration, fleet management, financing, insurance, compliance and maintenance. It is hardware-agnostic, so customers choose the robot best suited to each task. [C]

## Problem
In the founder's words, adoption fails on financing, risk and compliance long before it fails on technology. European factories also face labour shortages.

## Team
Alexander Stevens (founder and CEO). Other team members were not named in the coverage found so far.

## Round and traction
$2M pre-seed on 27 August 2026, led by Extantia Capital with Norrsken Evolve. Funds move five existing pilots into production and grow the robot fleet, starting in the Benelux. Five active pilots with industrial, warehouse and logistics companies in Belgium. [R]

## Why it is on the list
A services layer on top of the humanoid boom rather than another hardware bet. Buying robots instead of building them keeps capital needs lower.

## Open questions for the deep dive
Who owns the fleet and bears depreciation risk. Margin after robot cost, insurance and support. Pilot-to-production conversion. Dependence on third-party robot supply and pricing.
