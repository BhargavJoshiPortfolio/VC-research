## What it does
Electric impulse drilling: high-voltage pulses fracture rock from within rather than grinding it mechanically, targeting deep, hot granite. [C]

## Problem
Conventional drill bits wear out quickly at 5 to 20 km depth in hard, hot rock. Drilling can be up to 70% of a geothermal project's cost, which makes many projects uneconomic before they start. [C]

## Team
Philipp Engelkamp (CEO) and Andrew Welling (CTO). Founded 2025, built on about two decades of German university research.

## Round and traction
€4M pre-seed raised in autumn 2025 from Nucleus Capital, Possible Ventures and First Momentum, announced in March 2026. Demonstrated granite-breaking efficiency in research settings and holds a validation agreement with SPRIND, Germany's breakthrough-innovation agency. Market entry planned for 2026. [R]

## Why it is on the list
If drilling costs fall, superhot-rock geothermal becomes a baseload option, which is a large prize. US interest in next-generation geothermal is strong.

## Open questions for the deep dive
The distance from lab to field-scale drilling. Rate of penetration versus alternative approaches. Capital needs before revenue. The round is about a year old, so a seed raise may be near.
